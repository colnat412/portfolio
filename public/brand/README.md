# Colnat wordmark

Exported from the current portfolio wordmark: lowercase colnat with its signature green dot, DM Sans at weight 700, synthetic italic at 14 degrees, and -0.065em letter spacing.

## Files

- colnat-logo.svg: primary outlined vector, recommended master for long-term use
- colnat-logo.png: transparent PNG, 1600px wide
- colnat-logo@2x.png: transparent PNG, 3200px wide
- colnat-logo-monochrome.svg / .png: single-color ink version
- colnat-logo-light.svg / .png: white lettering and lime dot for dark backgrounds

SVG lettering is converted to paths: it needs no installed font and can be resized without losing sharpness. Keep the SVG as your master. All PNGs have a transparent background.

## Colors

Primary ink: #292D24
Signature dot: #647C29
Light version dot: #C8E877

Scale the logo proportionally and retain the signature dot.
